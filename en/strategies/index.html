Investment Strategies

Hengfeng Capital invests across equities, fixed income, infrastructure and credit, combining fundamental research, macroeconomic analysis and quantitative methods to assess opportunities across market environments.

OUR INVESTMENT APPROACH

Independent research.
Shared expertise.
Disciplined allocation.

HENGFENG CAPITAL · OUR APPROACH

Industry research, macroeconomic analysis and quantitative validation inform a common decision framework, with capital allocation governed by valuation, liquidity and risk budgets.

From Research to Allocation

Every opportunity must answer four questions: where value comes from, how reliable the evidence is, how much risk a portfolio can bear and what changes require action.

01 / INVESTMENT PROCESS

Research & Valuation

We combine company disclosures, industry discussions and site visits to assess business performance, industry structure and macroeconomic conditions. Research conclusions are compared with market-implied expectations to identify evidence that supports or challenges an investment view.

Site visits & industry research
Visit chip manufacturers and infrastructure suppliers to examine technology roadmaps, production and delivery capabilities, and end-market demand. Cross-check observations against public disclosures and industry data.
Earnings & cash flow
Distinguish earnings growth driven by demand, pricing or product mix from one-off effects. Assess capital expenditure, working-capital needs and cash generation to evaluate the durability of operating performance.
Macro scenarios
Use base and stress scenarios to assess how changes in growth, inflation, policy paths and funding costs affect earnings, valuations and asset pricing.
Expectations & valuation
Compare the research view with market expectations, specifying valuation methods, key assumptions and holding horizons. Assess potential returns and downside risks and identify business or market conditions that could change the conclusion.

DECISION USE

Distinguish direct observations, interview statements and analytical inferences. Record sources, key assumptions and open questions to provide a reviewable basis for valuation and capital allocation.

01 / FUNDAMENTAL EQUITY

Fundamental Equity

We combine public information, industry discussions and site visits to assess competitive advantages, industry dynamics and earnings quality. Valuation and cash-flow analysis help identify differences between market expectations and long-term business value.

02 / EQUITY ARBITRAGE

Equity Arbitrage

We combine fundamental analysis and quantitative tools to assess pricing differences between related securities, considering how events, hedging costs and liquidity affect relative-value opportunities.

03 / FIXED INCOME

Fixed Income

Grounded in macroeconomic research, we analyze rates, yield curves and bond pricing, assessing opportunities across markets in the context of policy paths, funding conditions and currency movements.

04 / INFRASTRUCTURE

Infrastructure

Across digital infrastructure, energy and utilities, we study demand durability, project economics and financing structures. Site visits to relevant suppliers inform our assessment of construction and delivery capabilities, capital expenditure and long-term cash flows.

05 / QUANTITATIVE STRATEGIES

Quantitative Strategies

We use data and models to research investment signals, applying out-of-sample validation, trading-cost analysis and portfolio assessment to test effectiveness and implementability across market conditions.

06 / CREDIT

Credit

We analyze creditworthiness and capital structures through corporate cash flows, debt terms and credit spreads, weighing compensation for risk against default exposure and recovery value.

PORTFOLIO & RISK

Assessing Every Investment in a Portfolio Context

From the initial research question to position reviews, we assess potential losses, shared risk drivers and liquidity constraints, defining position sizes, funding arrangements and exit conditions.

Risk First

Before assessing potential return, identify possible loss, sources of risk and how exposures may behave across market scenarios, avoiding reliance on a single view or market path.

Prudent Allocation

Align position size with holding horizon, funding arrangements, market depth and exit conditions so that a research view remains executable under real market constraints.

Ongoing Monitoring

Monitor key variables, portfolio risk exposures and the validity of investment assumptions, reassessing prior judgments as facts, prices or market structure change.
