Investment Strategies

Our research focuses on global macro and quantitative equity. We assess opportunities through asset pricing, the quality of the evidence and portfolio constraints.

01 / GLOBAL MACRO

Global Macro

Research in US and Chinese rates and currencies, focused on policy divergence and cross-market relative value.

Assess the drivers and durability of rate differentials, taking funding and hedging costs into account.

  • Rates & FX
  • Policy divergence
  • Relative value

02 / QUANTITATIVE EQUITY

Quantitative Equity

Systematic equity research across market regimes, signal structures and investment horizons.

Evaluate robustness through historical comparisons, out-of-sample testing and randomized benchmarks, alongside transaction costs and execution constraints.

  • Market regimes
  • Signal validation
  • Implementation

RISK MANAGEMENT

Portfolio Construction & Risk Management

Risk considerations apply across the research, assessment and implementation of both strategies.

Risk First

Weigh potential returns alongside possible losses, with an emphasis on diversification and risk control.

Prudent Allocation

Size investments with care, taking funding needs, investment horizons and market liquidity into account.

Ongoing Monitoring

Monitor markets and investment performance, regularly reassessing decisions and adapting as circumstances change.
