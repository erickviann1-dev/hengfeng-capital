Investment Strategies

Global macro and quantitative equity form our two research pillars. Each opportunity is assessed through pricing logic, quality of evidence, holding horizon, implementation costs and portfolio constraints.

01 / GLOBAL MACRO

Global Macro

Research across US and Chinese monetary policy, rate structures, currency pricing and cross-border capital flows to identify relative value across alternative policy paths.

Look beyond headline differentials to their drivers, durability and pace of change, incorporating funding, hedging, liquidity and invalidation scenarios into each assessment.

  • Rates & FX
  • Policy paths
  • Cross-border flows
Explore the research framework

02 / QUANTITATIVE EQUITY

Quantitative Equity

Systematic equity research spanning signal discovery, data integrity and holding-period analysis to assess return drivers and limits across market regimes.

Evaluate robustness through historical comparisons, out-of-sample tests, randomized benchmarks and version control, while incorporating turnover, transaction costs, capacity and portfolio exposures.

  • Market regimes
  • Signal validation
  • Horizon & implementation
Explore the research framework

PORTFOLIO & RISK

Turning Research Views into Disciplined Portfolios

Risk management is part of the research question, position sizing, implementation and exit conditions, rather than a check performed after an investment decision.

Risk First

Before assessing potential return, identify possible loss, sources of risk and how exposures may behave across market scenarios, avoiding reliance on a single view or market path.

Prudent Allocation

Align position size with holding horizon, funding arrangements, market depth and exit conditions so that a research view remains executable under real market constraints.

Ongoing Monitoring

Monitor key variables, portfolio exposures and invalidation conditions, reassessing prior judgments as facts, prices or market structure change.
