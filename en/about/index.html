About Us

Hengfeng Capital is an investment firm focused on global macro and quantitative equity research, connecting policy cycles, capital flows and market structure with independent judgment, portfolio constraints and ongoing review.

THE FIRM

A Global Perspective on Investing

We study the links between economic cycles, policy frameworks, funding conditions and asset pricing. Macro research helps explain what markets are pricing; quantitative analysis tests whether the evidence persists across samples, regimes and investment horizons.

A research conclusion becomes investable only when it can be translated into clear portfolio implications. We consider expected return, loss paths, liquidity, funding and hedging costs, and define in advance the conditions that could change the view.

We do not treat model output or a macro narrative as a final answer. Ongoing monitoring, version records and post-analysis review help test decision quality and keep research, portfolio construction and execution under a common discipline.

INSTITUTIONAL PERSPECTIVE

Investment Focus

Research Scope

Focused on global macro and quantitative equity across rates, currencies, equity markets and cross-border capital flows, with attention to relative value and sources of risk across market environments.

Decision Process

Begin with a defined research question, separate facts from assumptions and views, and form reviewable investment judgments through macro analysis, systematic validation and counter-scenarios.

Portfolio Discipline

Assess potential return alongside loss paths, exposures, liquidity, funding and implementation costs so portfolio decisions remain aligned with the evidence.
