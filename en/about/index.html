About Us

Hengfeng Capital is an investment firm focused on global macro and quantitative equity research, connecting policy cycles, capital flows and market structure with independent judgment, portfolio constraints and ongoing review.

THE FIRM

A Global Perspective on Investing

We study the links between economic cycles, policy frameworks, funding conditions and asset pricing. Macro research helps explain what markets are pricing; quantitative analysis tests whether the evidence persists across samples, regimes and investment horizons.

A research conclusion becomes investable only when it can be translated into clear portfolio implications. We consider expected return, loss paths, liquidity, funding and hedging costs, and define in advance the conditions that could change the view.

We do not treat model output or a macro narrative as a final answer. Ongoing monitoring, research-version records, decision attribution and ex-post validation help assess judgment quality and keep research, portfolio construction and execution under a common discipline.

INSTITUTIONAL EDGE

A Durable Decision Advantage for Complex Markets

The scarce resource is not an opinion, but the ability to turn insight into decisions that are testable, executable and continuously refined. Hengfeng Capital connects research, technology and risk discipline in one system designed to identify critical variables amid noise and preserve decision quality as markets change.

01

Integrated Research System

Global macro, quantitative research and portfolio engineering operate within one decision framework. Macro research identifies the variables driving markets, systematic methods test the strength of evidence, and portfolio research translates judgment into executable risk budgets.

02

Data & Technology Infrastructure

Structured data, reproducible experiments, cross-sample testing, scenario analysis and continuous monitoring form an iterative research workflow. Technology does not replace judgment; it improves its speed, consistency and auditability.

03

Portfolio Engineering & Risk

Every investment view is assessed against expected return, factor exposure, correlation, liquidity, market capacity, funding conditions and execution frictions, with stress scenarios and predefined invalidation conditions constraining the portfolio.

04

Selective Partnerships

We value partners who understand the process, respect discipline and measure the relationship over the long term. Shared standards, transparent communication and clear boundaries keep capital, research and decisions aligned.

SELECTIVE PARTNERSHIPS

We choose our partners by professional standards—and earn trust through long-term outcomes.

The most valuable partnerships begin with a shared understanding of research depth, risk boundaries and long-term objectives.
