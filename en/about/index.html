About Us

Hengfeng Capital is an investment management firm focused on global macro and quantitative equity research. We study how economic cycles, policy and capital flows shape asset prices, test investment hypotheses systematically, and carry those insights into portfolio construction and risk management.

THE FIRM

A Global Perspective on Investing

We study the links between economic cycles, policy frameworks, funding conditions and asset pricing. Macro research helps explain what markets are pricing; quantitative analysis tests whether research conclusions remain robust across samples, market regimes and investment horizons.

A research conclusion becomes investable when it can inform portfolio allocation. We consider expected return, loss paths, liquidity, funding and hedging costs, and define in advance the conditions that could change the view.

We do not treat model output or a macro narrative as a final answer. Ongoing monitoring, records of research assumptions and revisions, decision attribution and ex-post validation help assess judgment quality and keep research, portfolio construction and execution under a common discipline.

INSTITUTIONAL EDGE

A Durable Decision Advantage for Complex Markets

The scarce resource is not an opinion, but the ability to turn insight into decisions that are testable, executable and continuously refined. Hengfeng Capital connects research, technology and risk discipline in one system designed to identify critical variables amid noise and preserve decision quality as markets change.

01

Integrated Research System

Global macro, quantitative research and portfolio engineering operate within one decision framework. Macro research identifies the variables driving markets, quantitative methods test research hypotheses, and portfolio research translates judgment into executable risk budgets.

02

Data & Technology Infrastructure

Structured data, reproducible experiments, cross-sample testing, scenario analysis and continuous monitoring form an iterative research workflow. Technology does not replace judgment; it improves its speed, consistency and auditability.

03

Portfolio Construction & Risk

Every investment view is assessed against expected return, factor risk exposures, asset correlations, liquidity, strategy capacity, funding conditions and trading costs. Stress scenarios and predefined adjustment conditions guide portfolio risk controls.

04

Selective Partnerships

We value partners who understand the process, respect discipline and measure the relationship over the long term. Shared standards, transparent communication and clear boundaries keep capital, research and decisions aligned.

Our partnerships begin with a shared understanding of research standards, risk limits and long-term objectives.
