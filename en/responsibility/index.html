Purpose & Stewardship

Purpose defines what we pursue; stewardship defines how we proceed. We connect research standards, governance principles and verifiable action across capital allocation and the firm through the lens of long-term value.

OUR PURPOSE

Purpose sets the standard.Stewardship sustains the long term.

We believe enduring trust is built when judgment and action remain aligned. Environmental, social and governance factors are considered when material, alongside fundamentals, valuation, liquidity and portfolio constraints rather than as a separate label.

STEWARDSHIP IN PRACTICE

From Investment Judgment to Firm Governance

01

Investment Research

We identify governance, environmental and social factors that may affect long-term value and risk-return characteristics, assessing materiality by strategy, sector and investment horizon.

02

Firm Governance

Clear accountability, decision records and ongoing review support a consistent research and investment process and measured, verifiable communication.

03

Responsible Operations

Our operations emphasize professional conduct, information security, thoughtful resource use and responsibility to stakeholders.

OUR STANDARDS

Consistency Through Principles

Our aim is to form judgments that are grounded, reviewable and aligned with investment objectives amid incomplete information and changing conditions.

01

Materiality First

Focus on factors that can influence long-term value and risk decisions.

02

Evidence Led

Support judgments with verifiable information and distinguish facts, assumptions and views.

03

Decision Integration

Consider relevant factors alongside fundamentals, valuation, liquidity and portfolio constraints.

04

Ongoing Review

Reassess prior judgments as information and market conditions evolve.
