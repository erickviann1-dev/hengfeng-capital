Purpose & Stewardship

Independent minds.Enduring relationships.

We aspire to build a firm that stands the test of time.

Stay open to change, respect one another through disagreement and honor the trust others place in us. Our long-term ambitions are reflected in how we work together, take responsibility and approach every partnership.

WHAT WE VALUE

What matters to us

Shared values give people with different backgrounds and ideas a foundation for lasting collaboration.

01

Independent. Open-minded.

Think independently and take different perspectives seriously. We value reasoned disagreement, encourage questions and leave room to change our minds.

02

Candid. Respectful.

Speak clearly and discuss differences openly. Candor calls for courage and consideration. Respect for each person’s expertise and effort is where collaboration begins.

03

Accountable. Consistent.

Make commitments thoughtfully and follow through with care. Communicate difficulties early and take responsibility for shortcomings. Trust grows through consistent action.

HOW WE WORK TOGETHER

Different perspectives.Thoughtful conversations.

Good collaboration gives people space to speak independently and asks them to listen carefully. We want conversations to be candid and thorough, with ideas treated respectfully and examined thoughtfully.

We value enthusiasm for the work and consideration for the people doing it. Progress comes from sharing experience, acknowledging what we do not know and supporting one another when it matters.

RESPONSIBILITY, EVERY DAY

Responsibility.In everyday choices.

The character of a firm is shaped by the choices its people make every day.

01

Value trust

Be clear about what we can do and candid about limitations and uncertainty. Treat the confidence placed in us with care.

02

Respect boundaries

Respect the privacy of partners and colleagues, handle information carefully and uphold confidentiality and professional conduct.

03

Think beyond the present

Consider how today’s choices affect others and the future. Use time and resources thoughtfully, and support the firm’s development through learning and sound stewardship.
