PURPOSE & STEWARDSHIP

RISK & INVESTMENT DISCIPLINE

Understanding cycles through research.Navigating change with discipline.

Our approach to risk
01

Risk First

Before assessing potential return, identify possible loss, sources of risk and how exposures may behave across market scenarios, avoiding reliance on a single view or market path.

02

Prudent Allocation

Align position size with holding horizon, funding arrangements, market depth and exit conditions so that a research view remains executable under real market constraints.

03

Ongoing Monitoring

Monitor key variables, portfolio exposures and invalidation conditions, reassessing prior judgments as facts, prices or market structure change.
