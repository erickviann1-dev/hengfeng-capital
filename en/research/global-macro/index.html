Global Macro/Research Framework

Macro & FX Research

Research into the links between US and Chinese monetary policy, interest rates and currency pricing, with a focus on the funding, hedging and policy conditions that shape cross-market opportunities.

Research Focus

Rates & Funding

Examine cross-currency return structures through interest-rate curves and funding conditions. Assess headline differentials alongside holding costs, maturity mismatches and changes in access to funding.

Currencies & Hedging

Study the relationship between spot rates, forward pricing and hedging costs. Evaluate currency exposure and holding periods together when assessing whether a price dislocation remains meaningful after implementation costs.

Policy & Liquidity

Consider monetary-policy direction, balance-sheet adjustment and onshore and offshore funding conditions. Compare how alternative policy paths could change relative pricing.

Assessment & Limitations

A research view depends on rates, funding and hedging conditions together. Narrower differentials, higher funding costs or reduced liquidity may change the assessment. Historical relationships are not assumed to remain stable.
