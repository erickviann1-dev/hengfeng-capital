Quantitative Equity/Research Framework

Quantitative Equity Research

Systematic research into equity signals, market regimes and investment horizons. Historical comparisons and ongoing validation inform an assessment of the evidence and the conditions in which a strategy may be applicable.

Research Focus

Market Regimes

Examine how signal characteristics vary with trends, volatility and market structure. Results from a particular market episode are assessed in the context of the conditions that produced them.

Signal Validation

Use out-of-sample testing, randomized comparisons and version control to evaluate signal stability. Retain the basis for reviewing a conclusion and reduce reliance on any single historical period.

Horizon & Implementation

Assess holding periods, transaction costs and portfolio risk together. Examine whether a signal remains meaningful under practical execution constraints and how costs affect its return structure.

Assessment & Limitations

Historical signal performance does not establish an achievable portfolio outcome. Sample periods, holding horizons and execution costs affect the assessment. Changes in out-of-sample evidence or market structure call for a fresh review.
