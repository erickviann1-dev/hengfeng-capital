Macro Research/Market Judgment Record

Dollar Liquidity & RMB Assets

A research perspective on the Federal Reserve balance sheet, dollar funding conditions and China’s policy response. The page translates path analysis and falsification scenarios into key variables, portfolio implications and risk boundaries for investors.

Research Summary

Balance-Sheet Constraints

Examine the tension between monetary-policy direction and balance-sheet adjustment, with attention to the conditions governing dollar liquidity.

Cross-Market Transmission

Consider dollar funding conditions alongside China’s policy response to study the relationship between currencies, liquidity and RMB asset pricing.

Paths & Counter-Evidence

Consider path dependence in policy and compare alternative scenarios, including conditions that could alter or invalidate a research view.

Assessment & Limitations

The research examines how policy constraints change the conditions of asset pricing. Potential opportunities are considered alongside liquidity, implementation and risk limits.
