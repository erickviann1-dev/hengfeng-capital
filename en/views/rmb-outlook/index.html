RMB appreciation was more likely to persist at a managed pace

HENGFENG CAPITAL · RESEARCH PERSPECTIVE—APRIL 2026

Original Takeaway

Appreciation pressure on the RMB would persist, but the path would be managed around exports, employment and financial stability rather than replicate the yen’s rapid post-Plaza revaluation.

Renminbi Direction and the Pace of Adjustment

The USD/CNY fixing moved from 6.8674 on 24 April to 6.7195 on 22 October, consistent with a stronger renminbi over the period. These endpoints support the directional view. Assessing the pace and policy drivers requires the full time series and further evidence.

Selected from research written in April 2026, with review through October 2026. The observations are drawn from public market data. Views may change and do not constitute investment advice.
