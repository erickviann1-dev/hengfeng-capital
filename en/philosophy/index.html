Investment Philosophy

Understanding cycles through research. Navigating change with discipline. Our principles connect research evidence, portfolio decisions and ongoing review.

OUR INVESTMENT PRINCIPLES

Our Investment Principles

01

Independent Research

Start with an answerable question and distinguish facts, assumptions and views. Macro research explains the pricing context, quantitative analysis tests the strength of evidence, and counter-scenarios define when a view no longer holds.

02

Risk Awareness

Before discussing return potential, identify loss paths, correlation, concentration, liquidity and funding constraints, including whether these risks may emerge together under stress.

03

Investment Discipline

Define entry, adjustment, exit and invalidation conditions before investing, then record changing facts and portfolio responses to reduce narrative drift and hindsight justification.
