Investment Philosophy

A long-term perspective on technological change. Independent research to form judgment. Investment discipline to act with conviction. We believe AI and technological innovation are opening a new phase worthy of sustained research, with capital allocated around value creation and verifiable evidence.

OUR LONG-TERM CONVICTION

A View of the Future. A Discipline for the Present.

Our constructive outlook is rooted in a long-standing interest in technological progress and productivity. AI could reshape the economics of knowledge, computation and decision-making, changing how value is created through infrastructure, enterprise adoption and industrial upgrading. We believe this process is still early and warrants research across market cycles.

Every technology cycle involves gaps between expectations, capital investment and commercial delivery. Our work is to identify where technical advantages can become durable cash flows, assess the price markets attach to that potential and align holding horizons with the pace at which evidence emerges. Long-term conviction must be expressed through the quality of each allocation decision.

OUR INVESTMENT PRINCIPLES

Our Investment Principles

01

Focus on Structural Change

We believe AI is in an early phase of moving from technical breakthroughs to broad industrial adoption, with the potential to reshape the cost of knowledge work, business organization and returns on capital. Our research spans computing infrastructure, software and industry applications, asking who creates value, who captures profits and how much of that future is already priced.

02

Build Conviction through Research

Judgment should rest on evidence that can be challenged and tested. Macro research examines policy and capital conditions, industry analysis identifies business value, and quantitative methods test market evidence. We value corroboration across methods and the freedom to question consensus. Research gives patience a foundation and action a sense of proportion.

03

See through Noise. Assess Material Risk.

The intensity of a headline is not a measure of its effect on long-term value. We assess how geopolitical, trade and policy events transmit to supply chains, market access, energy costs and cash flows. We seek patience through volatility that leaves the thesis intact, and timely changes to assumptions and exposure when the underlying facts shift.

04

Align Price, Time & Discipline

A long-term direction requires an appropriate entry price, holding horizon and risk budget. We distinguish industrial progress, business earnings and market valuation, testing optimism against cash generation and returns on capital. Entry, resizing and exit decisions need explicit grounds so a portfolio can withstand waiting and respond when a thesis fails.

05

Partner with a Shared Long-term Perspective

We value partnerships grounded in professional understanding, independent thinking and a shared time horizon. Common research standards, clear risk boundaries and candid communication help a relationship endure changing sentiment and near-term disagreement. We seek partners who engage deeply with the method, respect investment discipline and share a commitment to long-term questions.
